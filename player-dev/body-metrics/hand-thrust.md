Measures how far the hands have moved away from the target line (depth) at the top of the backswing, viewed down the line.

## What It Measures
Quantifies the thrust distance of the hands away from the ball-target line at the TOP phase, measured down the line. Negative values indicate the hands have moved further from the target line and deeper behind the body; a more negative value means greater hand depth at the top.

## Target Windows by Goal
| Goal | Phase | Target | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hands Not Deep Enough | TOP | < -8.4" | Down The Line |

## Biomechanical Significance
Hand depth at the top of the backswing directly influences the swing plane and the club's delivery angle. When hands reach sufficient depth (< -8.4" from the ball-target line), the club is positioned behind the body and can drop onto an inside approach path naturally in the downswing. Shallow hands at the top constrain the club to a more outside approach, increasing the probability of a steep angle of attack, over-the-top move, or outside-in swing path.

## Fault Patterns
- **Not deep enough (> -8.4", i.e., less than 8.4" of depth):** Hands are too close to the target line at the top; swing plane is too steep or upright, predisposing over-the-top delivery and outside-in path.
- **Sufficient depth (< -8.4"):** Hands are positioned well behind the body at the top; club can shallow naturally into an inside attack angle.
