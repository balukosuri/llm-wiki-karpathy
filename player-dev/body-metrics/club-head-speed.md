Measures the speed of the club head at impact in miles per hour, the primary power output metric in SportsBox.

## What It Measures
Captures the velocity of the club head as it passes through the impact zone, measured face-on at the IMP phase. This is a scalar speed measurement (mph) and reflects the cumulative output of the entire kinematic chain — from ground force through pelvis, chest, arms, and wrists.

## Target Windows by Goal
| Goal | Phase | Target | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Increase Club Head Speed | IMP | > 74.0 mph | Face On |

## Biomechanical Significance
Club head speed is the single greatest contributor to distance, and improvements in speed directly expand carry and total distance options. Speed is generated through sequential rotation of the body segments (pelvis → chest → arms → club), efficient lag retention through the downswing, and explosive hip/chest clearing at impact. Deficiencies anywhere in this chain — such as early casting, insufficient pelvis turn, or lack of chest opening — will limit the speed ceiling regardless of effort.

## Fault Patterns
- **Below target (< 74.0 mph):** Insufficient power output — likely linked to breakdowns in the kinematic sequence such as early casting (see [[lead-wrist-angle]]), insufficient body rotation, or failure to shift weight to the lead side.
- **At or above target (≥ 74.0 mph):** Club head speed is within or above the target range for this goal; other quality metrics (path, face angle, impact location) determine ball flight outcome.
