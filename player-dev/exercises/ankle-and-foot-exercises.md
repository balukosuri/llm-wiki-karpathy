Ankle mobility and foot stability exercise library developing dorsiflexion range, plantar/dorsiflexor strength, and single-leg stability to support weight transfer, ground-force production, and postural control throughout the golf swing.
## What This Category Trains
**Ankle Mobility** exercises restore dorsiflexion and plantar flexion range so the lower extremity can accept load in a deep, centered squat pattern without compensating through heel rise, knee valgus, or early lumbar flexion. Limited ankle dorsiflexion is a common hidden driver of early extension and slide. **Foot Stability** exercises develop the neuromuscular control required to maintain a stable base under single-leg loading — essential during weight transfer, lead-side push-off, and the high-force moments of impact. Together, these two categories form the base of the kinetic chain through which all rotational power is expressed.

## How to Use This Library
Ankle mobility exercises belong in the **prep block**. Open with calf and tibialis foam roll (60 seconds per site), then progress through kneeling ankle mobilization drills before moving to squat-pattern work. Squat progressions (Bootstrap → Bodyweight Squat → Goblet Squat Prying → Overhead Squat) develop dorsiflexion under increasing load — use whichever level the player can achieve with neutral spine and full heel contact. Foot stability exercises (glute bridges, single-leg balance, lunge patterns) can be placed in the **main block** as primary training or used as activation sets before power or strength work. Pallof press and anti-rotation variations are best programmed in the **core/power block**. For players with overhead deep squat failures driven by ankle restriction, prioritize the kneeling ankle mobility series 5×/week before reassessing.
