Shoulder mobility and scapular stability exercise library addressing lat flexibility, external rotation range, and scapular control to support arm depth, wrist position, and safe loading throughout the golf swing.
## What This Category Trains
**Shoulder Mobility** exercises restore range of motion in the glenohumeral joint and shoulder girdle, with emphasis on internal/external rotation (90/90), overhead elevation (lat test), and horizontal adduction. Restricted shoulder mobility limits arm depth at the top, contributes to casting and scooping, and forces compensatory movements in the wrist and forearm. **Scapular Stability** exercises train the rotator cuff, mid/lower trapezius, and serratus anterior to maintain scapular position under load, which is critical for consistent arm position through impact and for protecting the shoulder during the high-velocity downswing.

## How to Use This Library
Shoulder mobility exercises belong in the **prep block**, sequenced after foam rolling and alongside thoracic spine work. Begin with supine/passive stretches (Floor Slides, Overhead Reach, Lat Stretch) to open range, then progress to active-assisted exercises (Pullaparts, Pass Throughs) that teach the shoulder to control its new range. Scapular stability exercises (Prone A/T/W) are better placed in the **main block** as activation work, typically 2–3 sets of 10–12 reps. The Driver/Dowel Overhead Tall Kneeling series bridges mobility and stability and works well at the end of the prep block or as a warm-up set for the main session. For players with a 90/90 screen failure, prioritize Side Lying External Rotation and Golf Club External Rotation daily until re-screen passes before advancing to compound resistance exercises.
