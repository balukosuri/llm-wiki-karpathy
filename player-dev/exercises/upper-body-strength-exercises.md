69 pull, push, carry, wrist, and upper body accessory exercises that develop upper body strength, shoulder stability, and the anti-rotation capacity required to transfer power efficiently through the golf swing.

## What This Category Trains
Pull patterns develop lat engagement, posterior chain strength of the lead arm, and the deceleration capacity needed through the follow-through. Push patterns build lead-side stability, overhead pressing strength for the full finish, and the rotational force production of the trail arm through impact. Carry exercises develop anti-lateral flexion strength and grip endurance. The rotational activation push exercises (Driver/Dowel pivot presses, lateral step punches) directly groove the pelvic-torso separation sequence required for maximum club head speed. Together, these patterns ensure the upper body can both generate and contain rotational force without compensatory posture loss.

## TPI Screens Addressed
Upper body strength exercises — specifically the rotational push activation exercises — target restrictions identified in:
- [[pelvic-rotation]] — the rotational driver push exercises train the hip-to-torso initiation sequence
- [[torso-rotation]] — rotational push and pull exercises load the thoracic spine through full rotation range
- [[lower-quarter-rotation]] — pivot press and lateral step punch patterns require lower limb rotational contribution
- [[seated-trunk-rotation]] — rotation-based exercises reinforce thoracic mobility under upper body load

When [[shoulder-external-rotation]] or [[ninety-ninety]] screens are restricted, prioritize posterior capsule work and low-load pull activation exercises before progressing to heavy loaded push movements.

## Golf Application
Upper body strength supports [[backswing-full-turn]] through lat and thoracic extensibility. Pull-pattern exercises with rotation (MB Rotational Slams, Cable Rotational Rows) develop the trail-side pulling force and deceleration pattern that mirrors the downswing kinetic chain. Push-pattern exercises with rotation (Landmine Rotational Clean & Press, Driver/Dowel Pivot Presses) directly transfer to the feel of lead-arm extension and trail-shoulder internal rotation through impact, supporting [[downswing-stop-early-casting]]. Carry exercises build the grip and forearm endurance that underpin club control through a round. Wrist curl exercises address forearm conditioning for [[wrist-hinge]] and [[wrist-flexion-extension]] health.

## Programming Notes
**Activation block:** Rotational push and pull activation exercises (Driver/Dowel Pivot Presses, RB Rotational Row, RB High Knee Lateral Step & Chop, Hollow Hold + Plate Pullover) belong in the warm-up or movement prep block (2–3 sets × 8–12 reps). The Driver/Dowel activation exercises are highest priority when [[pelvic-rotation]], [[torso-rotation]], or [[seated-trunk-rotation]] screen failures are present.

**Main strength block:** Horizontal push (Barbell Bench Press, 2 DB Bench Press) and vertical push (Barbell Push Press, 2 DB Overhead Press) provide the primary strength stimulus at 3–5 sets × 4–8 reps. Pull supersets with push (e.g. Bent Over Row + Bench Press) maintain posterior chain activation and shoulder joint balance.

**Rotational power block:** Medicine ball rotational exercises (MB Rotational Slams, MB Rotational Throw, MB Chop Slam) and Landmine Rotational Clean & Press are power-development exercises — programme at 3–4 sets × 4–6 reps at maximal intent before fatigue. These carry the highest golf specificity of any UB exercise and directly rehearse the rotational force delivery sequence.

**Carry and accessory:** Farmer Carry, Suitcase Carry, and Overhead Carry are finisher exercises — 3–4 sets × 20–40 m. Wrist curls and shoulder raises are prehab/maintenance work programmed at end of session, 2–3 sets × 12–15 reps.

**Shoulder health screen integration:** When [[shoulder-external-rotation]] or [[ninety-ninety]] screens are restricted, reduce horizontal pressing volume, eliminate overhead pressing until mobility improves, and add posterior capsule and Y-T-W work in the activation block.
