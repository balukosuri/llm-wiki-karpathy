Thoracic spine mobility exercise library targeting extension, rotation, and lateral flexion of the mid-back to enable full shoulder turn, proper shoulder plane, and efficient power transfer in the golf swing.
## What This Category Trains
Thoracic spine mobility exercises restore and expand the rotational and extension capacity of the T1–T12 vertebral segments. The thoracic spine is the primary rotational engine of the upper body; when it is stiff, golfers compensate with lumbar rotation (injury risk), reverse spine angle, over-the-top swing paths, or loss of posture. Adequate T-spine mobility allows the chest to turn deeply on the backswing and enables the kinematic sequence to flow from pelvis to chest to arms without energy leakage.

## How to Use This Library
Thoracic spine mobility work is best placed in the **prep block** immediately after foam rolling and before dynamic warm-up movement. Sequence from supine/prone (passive) to quadruped (loaded) to kneeling to standing to golf-posture exercises to progressively integrate the new range into sport-specific patterns. The Golf Posture Driver/Dowel exercises are particularly valuable as a bridge: they train thoracic rotation in exact setup posture, making the mobility directly transferable to ball-striking. For players with seated trunk rotation or torso rotation screen failures, prioritize Side Lying Thoracic Rotation and Quadruped Thread the Needle as the minimum effective dose (2–3 sets, 8–10 reps per side) before advancing to compound flows.
